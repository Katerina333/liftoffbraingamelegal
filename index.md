## Common questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad → tap **your name** → **Subscriptions** → **Liftoff** → **Cancel Subscription**. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. If you cancel during the 7-day free trial, at least 24 hours before it ends, you won't be charged anything.

### I bought a subscription but the levels are still locked.

Open Liftoff → tap the **gear** icon → **Restore purchases**. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

### I got a new iPhone. Will I lose my subscription?

No. Install Liftoff, then use **Restore purchases** as above. Your progress itself is stored on your device, so it moves to a new iPhone only through an iCloud or computer backup.

### How do I start over?

Open Liftoff → **gear** icon → **Reset all data**. This erases your progress, streak and test results on this device and cannot be undone.

### Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. Liftoff follows your iPhone's text size: open **Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size**, or **Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text**.

### Is Liftoff a medical app?

No. Liftoff is a brain-training game. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
